# Data rights

## Scope and evidence

The six TCGA LAML example inputs from
[maftools revision 015a4cf](https://github.com/PoisonAlien/maftools/tree/015a4cf8c69ba89a55a3fdcea911421509e9a198/inst/extdata)
and the three recipe outputs are identified in provenance. The Python project
already documents these source tables and their preparation. Its packaging
assessment relies on established upstream redistribution; this record does not
reinterpret the maftools software license as a license for all TCGA data.

[TCGA reuse guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/ccg/research/genome-sequencing/tcga/using-tcga-data)
provides source acknowledgment and responsible-use context. Public access and
existing mirrors alone do not establish the managed-hosting decision required
by this repository.

## Conditions

Credit maftools, the input producers, and the TCGA Research Network; retain
source revisions, input identities, and preparation descriptions. State that
results are based in whole or in part on data generated by the TCGA Research
Network. Preserve no-reidentification conditions and avoid implying clinical
fitness. The recipe repository's CC0 dedication does not relicense the data.

## Decision

Local-only: unresolved. Preparation and compatibility verification are complete,
but this new recipe has not established a managed-hosting decision covering all
six contributing inputs and the derived bundle. No distribution manifest is
declared. This does not change the Python project's existing packaging decision.
