# Data-rights review

## Scope

This review covers the exact Broad Institute GDAC Firehose archive and the two
unchanged members identified in `provenance.json`: `scores.gistic` and
`all_lesions.conf_99.txt`. The latter contains TCGA tumour-sample identifiers.

## Evidence and interpretation

The [Broad GDAC Firehose site](https://gdac.broadinstitute.org/) states that
downloading constitutes agreement to the TCGA data-usage policy, that the
primary data and analysis results are governed by TCGA responsible-use
guidelines, and that the site is copyrighted by the Broad Institute.

The NCI [TCGA citation guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/ccg/research/genome-sequencing/tcga/using-tcga-data/citing)
requests acknowledgment of the TCGA Research Network and says the
disease-specific publication moratoria have been lifted. The NCI
[TCGA human-subjects and data policies](https://www.cancer.gov/about-nci/organization/ccg/research/structural-genomics/tcga/history/policies/tcga-human-subjects-data-policies.pdf)
classify public data summaries, including copy-number alterations derived from
SNP analysis, as open-tier rather than controlled-tier data.

Those sources establish open access, responsible-use conditions, and
publication permission. They do not provide an explicit licence or other
clear grant for third parties to mirror byte-identical Broad-produced GISTIC
analysis files. A public download URL alone is insufficient redistribution
evidence. The repository's CC0 dedication does not apply to the data.

## Conditions

- Keep the accepted files local unless an authoritative redistribution grant
  for these exact analysis outputs is established.
- Identify the Broad Institute GDAC Firehose and TCGA OV cohort as sources.
- Include the requested acknowledgment: the results are based in whole or in
  part on data generated by the TCGA Research Network.
- Preserve TCGA responsible-use conditions and do not imply clinical fitness
  or endorsement.

## Decision

Local-only: redistribution rights for mirroring the exact archive members are
unresolved. No GenomeSpy-managed hosting target is proposed.
