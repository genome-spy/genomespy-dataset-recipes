# Data-rights review

## Scope

This review covers released ENCODE file
[ENCFF976OKL](https://www.encodeproject.org/files/ENCFF976OKL/) from annotation
[ENCSR627ANP](https://www.encodeproject.org/annotations/ENCSR627ANP/) and these
recipe outputs:

- `output/regulatory-element-gene-links.tsv.gz`, a near-complete transformed
  table with selected and renamed source columns;
- `output/candidate-regulatory-elements.tsv.gz`, a deduplicated derived table;
- `output/target-gene-tss.tsv.gz`, a deduplicated derived table.

## Evidence

- The authoritative
  [ENCODE data-use policy](https://www.encodeproject.org/about/data-use-policy/)
  makes released ENCODE data available for unrestricted use and describes rapid
  dissemination as a project goal.
- The ENCODE Portal identifies ENCSR627ANP as released ENCODE4 data and records
  the Jesse Engreitz lab at Stanford as the producing lab.
- [ENCODE citation guidance](https://www.encodeproject.org/help/citing-encode/)
  identifies the expected consortium, laboratory, dataset-accession, and
  file-accession credits.

## Interpretation

The unrestricted-use policy applies to this released ENCODE analysis product
and supports public redistribution of the documented transformed outputs. The
source is public functional-genomics data from the K562 cell line, not a
controlled-access dataset. No dataset-specific term contradicting the general
policy was found.

## Conditions

- Credit the ENCODE Consortium and the Jesse Engreitz lab at Stanford.
- Identify annotation ENCSR627ANP and file ENCFF976OKL.
- Link to the authoritative annotation, file, and data-use policy.
- Describe the hosted files as GenomeSpy recipe derivatives rather than
  original ENCODE files.
- Do not apply or imply the repository's CC0 dedication to any data file.

## Decision

Eligible for GenomeSpy-managed hosting. This decision records eligibility; it
does not claim that the outputs have already been uploaded.
