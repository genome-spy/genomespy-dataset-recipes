# Data-rights review

## Scope

This review covers released ENCODE file
[ENCFF976OKL](https://www.encodeproject.org/files/ENCFF976OKL/) from annotation
[ENCSR627ANP](https://www.encodeproject.org/annotations/ENCSR627ANP/) and the
three transformed TSV outputs listed in the recipe README.

## Evidence and interpretation

The authoritative [ENCODE data-use policy](https://www.encodeproject.org/about/data-use-policy/)
allows unrestricted use of released ENCODE data. The portal identifies this as
released ENCODE4 K562 data from the Jesse Engreitz lab at Stanford. No
dataset-specific restriction was found. ENCODE's
[citation guidance](https://www.encodeproject.org/help/citing-encode/) describes
the expected credits.

## Conditions

- Credit the ENCODE Consortium and the Jesse Engreitz lab at Stanford.
- Identify ENCSR627ANP and ENCFF976OKL and link to the source and policy.
- Describe the files as GenomeSpy-produced derivatives, not original ENCODE
  files.
- Do not imply that repository CC0 covers the data.

## Decision

Eligible for GenomeSpy-managed hosting under the conditions above.
