# Data-rights review

## Scope

A reduced hg38 chromosome 1 reference-sequence extract from UCSC, with
project-authored interval, control-site, and source metadata. No model outputs,
third-party annotation tracks, or patient observations are included.

## Evidence and interpretation

[UCSC's data terms](https://genome.ucsc.edu/license/) permit public and
commercial use of its raw data, including API access, subject to source-specific
restrictions. The [hg38 assembly README](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg38/bigZips/README.txt)
identifies GRCh38 and its chromosome sequence relationship to NCBI/EBI; it
states no additional restriction for this chromosome sequence. This is not a
restricted annotation track or a liftOver chain.

## Conditions

Credit UCSC Genome Browser and the Genome Reference Consortium. Retain the
source URL, assembly, coordinates, and notice that this is a reformatted
reference extract, not AlphaGenome predictions. The repository's CC0 dedication
does not license the reference sequence.

## Decision

Eligible for GenomeSpy-managed hosting under the conditions above.
